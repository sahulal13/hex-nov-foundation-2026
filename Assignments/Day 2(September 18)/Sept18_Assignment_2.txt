1.PreOrder Traversal:
	50 30 20 40 70 60 65 80

2.Inorder Traversal:
	20 30 40 50 60 65 70 80

3.Postorder Traversal:
	20 40 30 65 60 80 70 50

4. Inorder traversal gives the numbers in sorted order because the given tree is a binary tree where the left child of a node is always smaller than the node and the right child of the node is always greater than the node. Since inorder traversal performs traversal in the order of:
Left child -> Parent Node -> Right child.

We get the numbers in a sorted order.
